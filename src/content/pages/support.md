# Support HeatSync Labs

HeatSync Labs is 100% volunteer-run and funded by people like you. Here's how you can help keep the doors open and the tools running.

<a href="https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/donate-to-heatsync" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="donate-cta">Donate Now</a>

## Ways to Support Us

### Become a Member

The most impactful way to support HeatSync Labs is to [become a member](/membership). Members get access to storage, voting rights, and the satisfaction of directly supporting our mission.

### Donate Online

- **[Zeffy](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/donate-to-heatsync)**: Credit/debit cards, one-time or recurring. 100% goes to HeatSync.
- **[PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/donate?token=CHcahRyHOUjNz5wjbXa6yNOcYC-NDAoe3B_7Kz4fv1rmataOD0O3cW9WIp2IWjlS4m1mvorMP8kqQIUm)**: Pay with your PayPal balance or card. Small processing fee applies.
- **[Zelle](https://zellepay.com/)**: Send directly from your bank app to **finances@heatsynclabs.org**. No fees.

For a full breakdown of payment methods, see our [Ways to Pay](/pay) page.

### Cash or Check

Prefer to donate in person? Bring cash or a check to the lab during open hours. Make checks payable to "HeatSync Labs".

**Our Address:**
108 W Main St, Mesa, AZ 85201

### Shop Our eBay Store

Purchase items from our [eBay store](https://www.ebay.com/usr/heatsynclabs). We sell surplus equipment, member projects, and other interesting finds. Every purchase helps fund our operations.

### Equipment Donations

Have tools or equipment you no longer need? We accept donations of working tools, electronics, raw materials, and other items that benefit our community. Please reach out first to make sure we can use your donation.

### Corporate Sponsorship

Interested in corporate sponsorship? We offer various sponsorship levels for businesses that want to support maker education and innovation in Arizona. Contact us to learn more.

## Where Your Money Goes

- **Equipment**: Keeping our 3D printers, laser cutters, and workshop tools in working condition.
- **Facility**: Rent, utilities, and insurance for our 3,200 sq ft space.
- **Community Access**: Keeping the space open and accessible during public hours.
- **Education**: Classes, workshops, and educational programs for all skill levels.

HeatSync Labs exists because of supporters like you. Whether you become a member, make a donation, or simply spread the word, you're helping build something special. Thank you for being part of our community.
